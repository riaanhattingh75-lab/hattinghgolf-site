Golf days & membership

The Hattingh Golf app handles golf day listings, player registrations, group assignment and paid memberships (PayFast, ZAR).

Members

Find a day

Browse upcoming golf days, register or unregister, and see your groups when the organiser assigns them.

Organisers

Run the day

Create events, set formats and fees, auto-assign 4-ball groups with tee times, and keep a light CRM of players and sponsors.

Membership

Paid memberships

Monthly and annual plans are managed in the app with PayFast. Public site rates stay off until you confirm them.

Ask in the app (or on WhatsApp) for current membership options
